TrueFalse settings

The free hosted checker, or your own (see the project README).

What you check goes to your TrueFalse server, which uses AI, search and fact-checking services. Uploads are deleted within 15 minutes. Messages and answers are kept up to 24 hours, checked claims and reports up to 30 days. Image fingerprints (not the images) are kept with no end date. With Gemini's free tier, Google may also use what you send to improve its products.
